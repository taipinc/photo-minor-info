In this course, you will be introduced to critical concepts and theories present within contemporary practice. This course is designed to increase your awareness, knowledge and confidence in relating to contemporary concepts of photography through readings, discussions, presentations and writing assignments. The central aim of this course is to develop your skills in presenting and writing about contemporary practice as part of a constantly-evolving definition of photography.

*Open to: Undergraduate majors and minors in Photography. Some seats reserved for Majors only.*
