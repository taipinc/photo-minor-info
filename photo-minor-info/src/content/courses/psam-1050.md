In this course, we will explore photography as a visual language and learn the fundamental techniques of image-making with a focus on digital capture. Through assignments, suggested readings, and lectures, we will examine the methods and aesthetics of photography. This hands-on technical course will give us a solid understanding of digital capture and image editing using the most current software. We will experiment with different modes of photography to increase visual fluency and create a final project portfolio based on individual interests.

*Open to: All university undergraduate degree students, except Photography majors.*
