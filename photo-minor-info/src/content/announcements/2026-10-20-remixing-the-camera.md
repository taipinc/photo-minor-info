![Remixing the Camera — PSAM 3805, Spring 2027, Parsons School of Design](./images/remixing.gif)

In this studio course, students will design and tinker with digital cameras, remixing software and hardware, through which they will critically examine the fundamental technologies shaping contemporary photography. The course integrates critical theory with technical experimentation.

*Open to: All undergraduate degree students. Some seats reserved for Photo majors and minors. Pre-requisites: PUPH 2100 Core Studio 1 or PSAM 1050 Intro to Photography.*
