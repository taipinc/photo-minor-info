![Remixing the Camera — PSAM 3805, Spring 2027, Parsons School of Design](./images/remixing.gif)

Students in this studio class rethink digital cameras by modifying their hardware and software. Practical projects are paired with readings and conversations about computational photography and automated visual analysis, connecting experimentation with questions about culture, society, and politics.

By building and altering imaging tools, students investigate the values and inequalities those tools can carry. Their projects explore new uses for photography, challenge established imaging methods, and imagine cameras designed around a wider range of people and perspectives.
