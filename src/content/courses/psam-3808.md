In this Advanced Photo Processes course, we will explore how the photographic image operates across media, formats, and contexts. Integrating the still image with video, sound, installation, and performance you will develop your individual creative vision and conceptual approach to making. Through lectures, readings, discussions, and critique you will experiment, critically reflect, and ethically engage with the potentials of the photographic image. With an emphasis on conceptual practices and contemporary discourse you will build the foundation for a practice that fuses the photographic image with other media, extending the photo beyond the traditional print.

*Open to: All university undergraduate degree students. Pre-requisite(s): PUPH 2110 Core Studio 2, PSAM 1050 Photo Practices or equivalent. Some seats have been reserved for Bachelor of Photography majors.*
